01 — Features

Built like an employee.
Gated like a vault.

Every powerful capability sits behind a permission wall you control.

Stranger-proof by default

Unknown numbers are held at the door. You approve every user from your dashboard before the AI says a single word to them.

Google Workspace native

Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Gmail — connected once via OAuth, then just ask in chat. "What's on my plate Friday?"

One bot, many people

Your whole team texts the same number. Each person gets their own context, own permissions, own Google connection.

Remembers everything

Persistent memory across sessions. Preferences, projects, past decisions — it picks up where you left off, weeks later.

Plays nice in groups

In group chats it stays silent unless @mentioned. Privacy-first: no eavesdropping on conversations it isn't part of.

Your server, your data

Runs entirely on infrastructure you own — a single VPS is enough. Conversations never pass through someone else's cloud.

02 — How it works

Four minutes to a
full-time employee.

  1. 1

    Scan the QR

    Link your WhatsApp account right from the dashboard. No SIM swap, no new number — it rides on yours.

  2. 2

    One OAuth flow wires up Calendar, Drive, Gmail and Sheets. Revocable any time.

  3. 3
