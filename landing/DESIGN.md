# omniWA Landing — Design Contract

> THESIS: WhatsApp's own marketing voice — utility so confident it doesn't
> perform. Cream paper, black ink, one green pill. Refuses the dark-neon SaaS
> hero this category always ships.
> OWN-WORLD: #fcf5eb canvas floor, #1c1e21 ink at weight 400 everywhere,
> single #25d366 voltage pill per viewport, 50px/25px/50% radius scale,
> flat surfaces (no shadows), hand-drawn sketchbook annotations as the only
> decorative layer.
> STORY: omniWA is your WhatsApp employee — visitor sees a real 3D phone run
> an approval conversation while scrolling, understands the gate, clicks the
> green pill.
> FIRST VIEWPORT: cream field; left — 80px-class weight-400 headline with one
> hand-drawn green underline + green pill CTA; right — 3D phone (real
> extruded model) floating over faint blueprint grid.
> FORM: pinned WhatsApp-marketing direction, user-pinned brief.
> FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
> finish review, the verdict, and DESIGN.md.
